# Excel MIS & Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **Excel-based MIS and Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard** designed to monitor sales performance, target achievement, employee productivity, product performance, location-wise sales, returns, and key business KPIs.

The project demonstrates practical **Microsoft Excel and MIS reporting skills** relevant to Data Analyst and Business Analyst roles.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance and trends
- Compare actual sales against assigned targets
- Evaluate employee-wise sales performance
- Analyze product-wise and location-wise sales
- Track returns and return amounts
- Monitor working hours and attendance-related metrics
- Identify performance gaps and opportunities for improvement
- Provide management-friendly KPI reporting through an Excel dashboard

## 📋 Analysis Areas

### 1. Daily Sales Analysis
Tracks sales activity and helps identify daily sales patterns and performance.

### 2. Target vs Achievement
Compares actual sales with assigned targets and highlights achievement gaps.

### 3. Employee Performance
Evaluates individual employee sales performance and contribution.

### 4. Location-wise Sales
Analyzes sales performance across locations to identify strong and weak markets.

### 5. Product-wise Sales
Identifies high-performing products and products requiring further attention.

### 6. Return Analysis
Monitors return quantities and return amounts to understand their impact on net sales.

### 7. Monthly Sales Summary
Provides a consolidated view of monthly performance and important business KPIs.

## 📊 Dashboard

The Excel dashboard brings together key metrics and analysis into a management-friendly view, making it easier to:

- Monitor business performance
- Compare targets with actual results
- Identify trends
- Find underperforming areas
- Support data-driven business decisions

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The analysis can be used to identify:

- Employees exceeding or falling short of sales targets
- Products generating the highest sales
- Locations contributing the most revenue
- Target achievement gaps
- Return patterns affecting net sales
- Monthly sales trends
- Areas requiring operational or sales improvement
